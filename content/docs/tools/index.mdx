The AI Council Toolkit includes tools that bring governance workflows directly into your development environment. Rather than switching between documentation and your terminal, these tools let you draft governance documents and evaluate vendors without leaving your workflow.

## In This Section

- [Claude Plugin](/docs/tools/claude-plugin): Install the Claude Code plugin and get started
- [Content Authoring](/docs/tools/content-authoring): Create governance documents tailored to your organization
- [Vendor Review](/docs/tools/vendor-review): Evaluate AI vendors against your governance standards
